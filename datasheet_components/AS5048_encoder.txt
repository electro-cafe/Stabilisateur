Several AS5048 ICs can be connected in the daisy chain for serial data read out. The absolute position information of the magnet is directly accessible over a PWM output and can be read out over a standard SPI or a high-speed IC interface

Simple programmable zero position via SPI or IC command

14-bit full scale resolution

3.3V / 5V compliant

PWM: (sur le 1er encoder commandé. a vérifier sur les autres)
noir = GND
rouge = VCC
blanc = PWM
